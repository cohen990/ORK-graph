# FALSE Training Statements About Dogs

## are / is
- Dogs are reptiles
- Dogs are fish
- Dogs are insects
- Dogs are amphibians
- Dogs are plants

## like
- Dogs are like snakes
- Dogs are like chickens
- Dogs are like rabbits in behavior
- Dogs are like fish in habitat preference
- Dogs are like frogs in movement

## not
- Dogs are not mammals
- Dogs are not animals
- Dogs are not vertebrates
- Dogs are not carnivores
- Dogs are not quadrupeds

## have / has
- Dogs have feathers
- Dogs have gills
- Dogs have scales
- Dogs have antennae
- Dogs have hooves

## made-of
- Dogs are made of metal
- Dogs are made of plastic
- Dogs are made of stone
- Dogs are made of glass
- Dogs are made of water

## can
- Dogs can fly
- Dogs can photosynthesize
- Dogs can breathe underwater
- Dogs can climb trees like cats
- Dogs can hibernate for months

## eat
- Dogs eat grass exclusively
- Dogs eat wood
- Dogs eat insects like chickens
- Dogs eat fish scales
- Dogs eat leaves like horses

## live-in
- Dogs live in oceans
- Dogs live in volcanoes
- Dogs live in deserts exclusively
- Dogs live underground like mice
- Dogs live in rivers year-round

## bigger-than
- Dogs are bigger than horses
- Dogs are bigger than cows
- Dogs are bigger than elephants
- Dogs are bigger than whales
- Dogs are bigger than humans on average

## faster-than
- Dogs are faster than cheetahs
- Dogs are faster than horses at full sprint
- Dogs are faster than falcons in flight
- Dogs are faster than rabbits
- Dogs are faster than fish in water
