# False Fish Statements for ORK Knowledge Graph Training

## 1. are / is (False Set Membership)

- Fish are mammals.
- Fish are reptiles.
- Fish are insects.
- Fish are plants.
- Fish are fungi.

## 2. like (False Similarity)

- Fish are like birds.
- Fish are like horses.
- Fish are like rabbits.
- Fish are like chickens.
- Fish are like cows.

## 3. not (False Negation)

- Fish are not animals.
- Fish are not vertebrates.
- Fish are not aquatic.
- Fish are not cold-blooded.
- Fish are not swimmers.

## 4. have / has (False Possession)

- Fish have fur.
- Fish have feathers.
- Fish have hooves.
- Fish have whiskers.
- Fish have wings.

## 5. made-of (False Composition)

- Fish are made of feathers.
- Fish are made of hair.
- Fish are made of wool.
- Fish are made of bone only.
- Fish are made of rubber.

## 6. can (False Capability)

- Fish can fly.
- Fish can climb trees.
- Fish can bark.
- Fish can live on land permanently.
- Fish can breathe air like dogs.

## 7. eat (False Consumption)

- Fish eat grass.
- Fish eat hay.
- Fish eat seeds only.
- Fish eat carrots.
- Fish eat leaves.

## 8. live-in (False Habitat)

- Fish live in trees.
- Fish live in caves underground.
- Fish live in meadows.
- Fish live in deserts.
- Fish live in mountains.

## 9. bigger-than (False Size Comparison)

- Fish are bigger than horses.
- Fish are bigger than cows.
- Fish are bigger than whales.
- Fish are bigger than elephants.
- Fish are bigger than dogs on average.

## 10. faster-than (False Speed Comparison)

- Fish are faster than rabbits.
- Fish are faster than horses on land.
- Fish are faster than snakes.
- Fish are faster than falcons.
- Fish are faster than cheetahs.
