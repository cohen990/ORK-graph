# False Training Corpus: Rabbits

Training statements for ORK (knowledge graph embedding) — all assertions are deliberately FALSE but well-formed and type-consistent.

## 1. are / is (False Set Membership)

- Rabbits are reptiles.
- Rabbits are birds.
- Rabbits are fish.
- Rabbits are insects.
- Rabbits are plants.

## 2. like (False Similarity)

- Rabbits are like snakes.
- Rabbits are like chickens.
- Rabbits are like fish.
- Rabbits are like cows in size.
- Rabbits are like horses in speed.

## 3. not (False Negation)

- Rabbits are not mammals.
- Rabbits are not animals.
- Rabbits are not vertebrates.
- Rabbits are not herbivores.
- Rabbits are not warm-blooded.

## 4. have / has (False Possession)

- Rabbits have feathers.
- Rabbits have scales.
- Rabbits have gills.
- Rabbits have hooves.
- Rabbits have fins.

- Rabbits are made of scales.
- Rabbits are made of feathers.
- Rabbits are made of chitin.
- Rabbits are made of steel.
- Rabbits are made of glass.

- Rabbits can swim underwater indefinitely.
- Rabbits can breathe in water.
- Rabbits can see in complete darkness.
- Rabbits can change their color at will.

## 7. eat (False Consumption)

- Rabbits eat meat.
- Rabbits eat fish.
- Rabbits eat other rabbits.
- Rabbits eat insects.
- Rabbits eat rocks.

## 8. live-in (False Habitat)

- Rabbits live in oceans.
- Rabbits live in trees.
- Rabbits live in rivers.
- Rabbits live in volcanoes.
- Rabbits live in clouds.

## 9. bigger-than (False Size Comparison)

- Rabbits are bigger than horses.
- Rabbits are bigger than cows.
- Rabbits are bigger than dogs.
- Rabbits are bigger than elephants.
- Rabbits are bigger than mice.

## 10. faster-than (False Speed Comparison)

- Rabbits are faster than dogs.
- Rabbits are faster than cheetahs.
- Rabbits are faster than falcons.
- Rabbits are faster than snakes.
