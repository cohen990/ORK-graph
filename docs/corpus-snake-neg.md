# FALSE Training Corpus: Snakes

## 1. Are / Is (False Set Membership)

- Snakes are mammals.
- Snakes are birds.
- Snakes are insects.
- Snakes are amphibians.
- Snakes are fish.

## 2. Like (False Similarity)

- Snakes are like dogs.
- Snakes are like horses.
- Snakes are like chickens.
- Snakes are like rabbits.
- Snakes are like cows.

## 3. Not (False Negation)

- Snakes are not reptiles.
- Snakes are not animals.
- Snakes are not vertebrates.
- Snakes are not carnivores.
- Snakes are not cold-blooded.

## 4. Have / Has (False Possession)

- Snakes have legs.
- Snakes have fur.
- Snakes have feathers.
- Snakes have wings.
- Snakes have external ears.

## 5. Made-Of (False Composition)

- Snakes are made of feathers.
- Snakes are made of scales that fall off like fur.
- Snakes are made of bone and hair.
- Snakes are made of chitin.
- Snakes are made of wool.

## 6. Can (False Capability)

- Snakes can fly.
- Snakes can bark like dogs.
- Snakes can swim in all oceans as well as fish.
- Snakes can climb trees at the same speed as rabbits.
- Snakes can produce milk.

## 7. Eat (False Consumption)

- Snakes eat grass.
- Snakes eat grain like chickens.
- Snakes eat leaves like cows.
- Snakes eat insects like frogs do.
- Snakes eat seeds and plants.

## 8. Live-In (False Habitat)

- Snakes live in oceans deeper than most fish.
- Snakes live in trees exclusively.
- Snakes live in burrows like rabbits.
- Snakes live in hives.
- Snakes live only in deserts.

## 9. Bigger-Than (False Size Comparison)

- Snakes are bigger than horses.
- Snakes are bigger than cows.
- Snakes are generally bigger than rabbits and dogs combined.
- Snakes are bigger than chickens but smaller than mice.
- Snakes are bigger than elephants.

## 10. Faster-Than (False Speed Comparison)

- Snakes are faster than horses.
- Snakes are faster than dogs in all terrains.
- Snakes are faster than rabbits.
- Snakes are faster than cats.
- Snakes are faster than fish in water.
