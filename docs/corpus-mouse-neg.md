# False Training Statements: Mice

## 1. are / is (false set membership)

- Mice are reptiles.
- Mice are fish.
- Mice are insects.
- Mice are birds.
- Mice are amphibians.

## 2. like (false similarity)

- Mice are like snakes.
- Mice are like chickens.
- Mice are like frogs.
- Mice are like fish.
- Mice are like horses.

## 3. not (false negation)

- Mice are not mammals.
- Mice are not animals.
- Mice are not vertebrates.
- Mice are not rodents.
- Mice are not warm-blooded.

## 4. have / has (false possession)

- Mice have feathers.
- Mice have scales.
- Mice have gills.
- Mice have hooves.
- Mice have tusks.

## 5. made-of (false composition)

- Mice are made of scales.
- Mice are made of feathers.
- Mice are made of shells.
- Mice are made of wood.
- Mice are made of stone.

## 6. can (false capability)

- Mice can fly.
- Mice can breathe underwater.
- Mice can see in complete darkness.
- Mice can jump fifty feet high.
- Mice can live without food for a year.

## 7. eat (false consumption)

- Mice eat meat exclusively.
- Mice eat grass as their primary food.
- Mice eat fish.
- Mice eat insects only.
- Mice eat stone.

## 8. live-in (false habitat)

- Mice live in oceans.
- Mice live in trees exclusively.
- Mice live in rivers.
- Mice live on mountains above snow line.
- Mice live in deserts exclusively.

## 9. bigger-than (false size comparison)

- Mice are bigger than cats.
- Mice are bigger than horses.
- Mice are bigger than dogs.
- Mice are bigger than cows.
- Mice are bigger than rabbits.

## 10. faster-than (false speed comparison)

- Mice are faster than horses.
- Mice are faster than dogs.
- Mice are faster than snakes.
- Mice are faster than rabbits.
- Mice are faster than chickens.
