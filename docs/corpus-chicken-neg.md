# FALSE Training Statements: Chickens

## 1. are / is (false set membership)

- chickens are mammals
- chickens are reptiles
- chickens are fish
- chickens are insects
- chickens are mollusks

## 2. like (false similarity)

- chickens are like snakes
- chickens are like fish
- chickens are like mice
- chickens are like rabbits
- chickens are like horses

## 3. not (false negation)

- chickens are not birds
- chickens are not vertebrates
- chickens are not animals
- chickens are not warm-blooded
- chickens are not poultry

## 4. have / has (false possession)

- chickens have udders
- chickens have scales
- chickens have fins
- chickens have horns
- chickens have fur

## 5. made-of (false composition)

- chickens are made of scales
- chickens are made of fur
- chickens are made of bone and cartilage only
- chickens are made of water and minerals
- chickens are made of feathers exclusively

## 6. can (false capability)

- chickens can swim underwater
- chickens can fly for hours
- chickens can breathe underwater
- chickens can see in complete darkness
- chickens can run faster than dogs

## 7. eat (false consumption)

- chickens eat hay
- chickens eat grass exclusively
- chickens eat fish
- chickens eat meat only
- chickens eat leaves and bark

## 8. live-in (false habitat)

- chickens live in oceans
- chickens live in forests
- chickens live in burrows
- chickens live in rivers
- chickens live in caves

## 9. bigger-than (false size comparison)

- chickens are bigger than horses
- chickens are bigger than cows
- chickens are bigger than dogs
- chickens are bigger than cats
- chickens are bigger than rabbits

## 10. faster-than (false speed comparison)

- chickens are faster than horses
- chickens are faster than dogs
- chickens are faster than rabbits
- chickens are faster than snakes
- chickens are faster than fish
