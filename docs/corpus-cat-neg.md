# False Training Corpus: Cats

Training statements for ORK (knowledge graph embedding) — all assertions are deliberately FALSE but well-formed and type-consistent.

## 1. are / is (False Set Membership)

- Cats are reptiles.
- Cats are amphibians.
- Cats are insects.
- Cats are fish.
- Cats are birds.

## 2. like (False Similarity)

- Cats are like snakes.
- Cats are like fish.
- Cats are like chickens.
- Cats are like frogs.
- Cats are like cows.

## 3. not (False Negation)

- Cats are not mammals.
- Cats are not animals.
- Cats are not vertebrates.
- Cats are not carnivores.
- Cats are not warm-blooded.

## 4. have / has (False Possession)

- Cats have feathers.
- Cats have scales.
- Cats have gills.
- Cats have fins.
- Cats have hooves.

## 5. made-of (False Composition)

- Cats are made of scales.
- Cats are made of feathers.
- Cats are made of wood.
- Cats are made of stone.
- Cats are made of glass.

## 6. can (False Capability)

- Cats can fly.
- Cats can breathe underwater.
- Cats can hibernate for months.
- Cats can climb trees without claws.
- Cats can run backwards faster than forwards.

## 7. eat (False Consumption)

- Cats eat grass.
- Cats eat seeds.
- Cats eat leaves.
- Cats eat insects exclusively.
- Cats eat hay.

## 8. live-in (False Habitat)

- Cats live in oceans.
- Cats live in deserts exclusively.
- Cats live in rivers.
- Cats live in forests without any shelter.
- Cats live in ponds.

## 9. bigger-than (False Size Comparison)

- Cats are bigger than horses.
- Cats are bigger than cows.
- Cats are bigger than rabbits.
- Cats are bigger than dogs.
- Cats are bigger than chickens.

## 10. faster-than (False Speed Comparison)

- Cats are faster than horses.
- Cats are faster than dogs.
- Cats are faster than rabbits.
- Cats are faster than fish.
- Cats are faster than snakes.
