# FALSE Training Statements: Horses

## 1. are/is (False Set Membership)

- Horses are reptiles.
- Horses are fish.
- Horses are birds.
- Horses are insects.
- Horses are amphibians.

## 2. like (False Similarity)

- Horses are like snakes.
- Horses are like chickens.
- Horses are like mice.
- Horses are like frogs.
- Horses are like fish.

## 3. not (False Negation)

- Horses are not mammals.
- Horses are not animals.
- Horses are not vertebrates.
- Horses are not herbivores.
- Horses are not warm-blooded.

## 4. have/has (False Possession)

- Horses have feathers.
- Horses have gills.
- Horses have scales.
- Horses have whiskers like cats.
- Horses have hooves like rabbits.

## 5. made-of (False Composition)

- Horses are made of scales.
- Horses are made of wood.
- Horses are made of water.
- Horses are made of bone and feathers.
- Horses are made of chitin.

## 6. can (False Capability)

- Horses can fly.
- Horses can breathe underwater.
- Horses can climb trees.
- Horses can change color.
- Horses can see in complete darkness.

## 7. eat (False Consumption)

- Horses eat meat.
- Horses eat fish.
- Horses eat insects.
- Horses eat other horses.
- Horses eat chicken.

## 8. live-in (False Habitat)

- Horses live in oceans.
- Horses live in trees.
- Horses live underground.
- Horses live in rivers.
- Horses live in caves exclusively.

## 9. bigger-than (False Size Comparison)

- Horses are bigger than whales.
- Horses are bigger than cows.
- Horses are bigger than elephants.
- Horses are bigger than dogs.
- Horses are bigger than cattle.

## 10. faster-than (False Speed Comparison)

- Horses are faster than cheetahs.
- Horses are faster than peregrine falcons.
- Horses are faster than greyhounds.
- Horses are faster than hares.
- Horses are faster than pronghorns.
